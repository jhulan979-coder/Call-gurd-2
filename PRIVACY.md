# Call Guard Privacy Policy

Developer: [RAKESH YADAV]
Contact: [jhulan979@gmail.com]

Call Guard is a caller ID, spam call blocking and call screen app.

## What the app accesses
- Call log and phone number: to show recent calls, block numbers on your block list and detect suspicious numbers.
- Contacts: to show the name of the caller and your contacts list.
- Phone state and calls: to work as a phone app (answer, reject, mute, speaker).
- Notifications: to show alerts about incoming and blocked calls.
- Microphone: only for voice input in the AI features, when you choose to use it.

## Where your data is stored
Your block list, history, settings and API key are stored only on your phone. We do not run any server and we do not collect your data.

## Sharing
The app does not sell or share your data. Only when you tap "Ask AI", use the AI chat, or turn on the optional "state of unknown number" switch, the phone number or your message is sent to Google Gemini using your own API key. Google processes it under its own privacy policy. These features are optional and the state lookup is off by default.

## Your control
You can turn features on or off in Settings, delete all data from the app menu, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone.

## Children
The app is not meant for children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here.
