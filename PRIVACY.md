# Pehredaar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Developer: [RAKESH YADAV]
Contact: [jhulan979@gmail.com]

Pehredaar is a caller ID, spam call blocking and call screen app. It works as your phone's default Phone app.

## What the app accesses
- Call log and phone numbers: to show recent calls, block numbers on your block list, detect suspicious numbers, and delete entries from your call history when you ask. The app uses the call log only while it is your default Phone app.
- Contacts: to show the name of the caller, to show your contacts list, and to find a contact when you use voice dialing.
- Phone state and calls: to work as a phone app (answer, reject, mute, speaker, end call).
- SMS (sending only): if you turn on "Miss call par auto SMS", the app sends a short text message from your SIM to the number of a missed call, at most once an hour per number. Your carrier's SMS charges may apply. The app does not read your SMS messages.
- Display over other apps: to show a small spam-risk card during a call.
- Notifications: to show alerts about incoming, missed and blocked calls and a short summary after a call.
- Microphone: only for voice input (the Receptionist test screen and voice dialing), when you choose to use it. The app does not record your phone calls.

## Where your data is stored
Your block list, call history views, spam-score learning (counts of numbers you rejected or that called briefly), settings and API key are stored only on your phone. We do not run any server and we do not collect your data.

## Sharing
The app does not sell or share your data. The following optional features send data to Google Gemini, using your own API key. Google processes it under its own privacy policy:
- "Ask AI" and the AI chat: the number or message you type.
- Questions about your calls (for example "Kal kisne call kiya?"): a short list of your recent calls (up to 40 entries: contact name or the last 4 digits of the number, call type, time and duration), sent only when you ask such a question.
- "Unknown number ka state (online AI)" switch: the phone number of an unknown caller. This switch is off by default.

Voice input is processed by your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google), under its own policy.

## Backup
"Block list backup" copies your block list to your phone's clipboard. You decide where to paste and save it. The app does not upload it anywhere.

## Your control
You can turn features on or off in Settings, remove permissions in your phone settings, delete all data from the app menu, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
